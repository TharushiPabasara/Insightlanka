# InsightLanka

### Cloud-Based Business Analytics & Multi-Branch Retail Synchronization SaaS

InsightLanka is a proposed cloud-based business analytics and multi-branch retail synchronization platform designed to support Sri Lankan small and medium-sized retail businesses.

The platform aims to provide centralized inventory visibility, business analytics, sales reporting, and synchronization across multiple retail branches.

## Project Overview

This project is developed as part of the **IE3111 – Organizational Functions** module at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT).

InsightLanka focuses on addressing challenges faced by multi-branch retail businesses, including fragmented inventory records, limited visibility across branches, stock shortages, and inefficient business decision-making.

## Main Objectives

- Centralize inventory information across multiple branches.
- Improve visibility of sales and stock movements.
- Provide business analytics and reporting.
- Support data integration through APIs and CSV/Excel uploads.
- Improve inventory management and replenishment decisions.
- Support data-driven decision-making for retail businesses.

## Proposed Key Features

- Multi-branch inventory synchronization.
- Centralized business analytics dashboard.
- Sales and inventory reporting.
- CSV and Excel data ingestion.
- POS and third-party API integrations.
- Automated stock alerts and replenishment recommendations.
- Cloud-based multi-tenant architecture.

## Target Market

Sri Lankan retail and pharmacy chains operating between 2 and 15 branches.

## Technologies and Architecture

The proposed solution considers:

- Cloud infrastructure: AWS or Microsoft Azure
- Architecture: API-first, multi-tenant client-server architecture
- Data ingestion: REST APIs, CSV and Excel uploads
- Data processing: ETL pipelines
- Security: Role-based access control and data encryption
